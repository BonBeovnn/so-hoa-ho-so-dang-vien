  Dong cua so do la tat ung dung.

* Neu trinh duyet bao "Phien khong hop le": quay lai cua so den,
  copy dong dia chi http://127.0.0.1:8000/... roi dan vao trinh duyet.

* Ung dung KHONG BAO GIO xoa hay doi ten tep cua ban.
  Thao tac duy nhat voi thu muc scan la CHEP RA.


TAM BUOC
--------
0. Thong tin don vi  - ten dang bo va ma dang bo co so [2].[3].[3]
                       LAM MOT LAN. App khong khoa cung vao don vi nao.
1. Nap du lieu       - chon DS_DANGVIEN.xlsx, dien ma to chuc dang tung chi bo
2. Duyet du lieu     - xem bang roi ghi so cai MAIN.xlsx
3. Tao cay thu muc   - chon thu muc goc, tao cay 4 cap
4. Quet tep scan     - phan loai hop le / loi, sua ten sai ngay tai cho
5. Luan chuyen tep   - xem truoc roi chep + doi ten, ghi manifest
6. Doi soat          - dem 104 loai tai lieu, ghi 6 cot vao MAIN.xlsx
7. Bao cao           - xuat .docx dung the thuc va phu luc .xlsx

Buoc sau chi mo khi buoc truoc da xong.


TEN TEP SCAN PHAI DAT THE NAO
-----------------------------
Chi can BA PHAN:

    A.ID01.65.pdf       hoac     ID01.65.pdf

    A     = ma chu cai chi bo   (CO THE BO)
    ID01  = ma dang vien, tra o sheet DSTTHC trong MAIN.xlsx
    65    = ma loai tai lieu 1..104, tra o sheet DANH_MUC_FILE

Muon tu chon thu tu thi them so o cuoi (CUNG CO THE BO):

    A.ID01.65.1.pdf     hoac     ID01.65.1.pdf

Khong ghi so thu tu thi app tu cap so noi tiep.

Duoi tep nhan: .pdf .doc .docx .jpg .jpeg .png
Tep khong phai PDF van duoc doi ten dung chuan nhung xep rieng vao
thu muc _CHO_CHUYEN_PDF.
